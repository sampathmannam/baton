## TL;DR

The v1.9.4 "horizontal siblings" DecayRow still had a per-row **Mark recent TextButton** that ate ~80dp of horizontal space, which forced the days-quiet text to ellipsize as "haven't touched in 93 d...". v1.9.5 drops the per-row button and replaces it with two gestures:

- **Swipe right** past 96dp → mark recent + Snackbar "Mark recent. [Undo]"
- **Long-press** → ModalBottomSheet with "Mark as recent" + "Cancel"

Result: days-quiet text renders in full ("haven't touched in 93 days") and the card is more glanceable. The status pill stays as the only visible right-side control.

## Trade-off vs the plan's success criterion

The plan said "6+ full DecayRow cards visible on first paint". The actual is **5 full + 1 partial** on the 1080×2400 emulator with the redistribute banner showing. The redistribute banner's icon + label eats ~50dp vertically.

The trade-off the implementer made was: full text on the existing 5 cards (was the user's "93 d..." complaint) instead of more cards total. With the redistribute banner hidden (fewer than 5 quiet contacts), more cards would fit. The user-visible goal — readable days-quiet text — is fully achieved. The 6+ count is a soft target that the redistribute banner eats; v1.9.6 can ship a "compact redistribute chip" to recover the extra card.
