# Baton v1.7.0 — Real-user test handoff

**Audience:** One outside user. Not the developer. Not a tester. A real
person who has never seen Baton before today.

**Goal:** Observe where the user hesitates, taps the wrong thing, asks a
question out loud, or gives up. The output is a list of friction points,
not a pass/fail.

**Time budget:** 30 minutes, no more. If they finish early, stop. Do
not push for "one more thing".

**Recording:** Screen-record the phone from the moment you hand it
over. Do not narrate while they are using the app. Take timestamped
notes after.

---

## Before you hand the phone over

1. Install the v1.7.0 APK on the test phone. The APK is
   `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/apk-debug.apk` after a successful
   `./gradlew.bat :app:assembleDebug`. The package is
   `com.baton.app.debug`.
2. Sign in with the tester's Supabase account (or skip sign-in and
   use the app as a guest — the vault-mode toggle in Settings decides
   whether data is hidden, not whether the app is usable).
3. Seed at least 3 people and 6 instructions so the search and
   per-person list have real data. Easy way: open the app on the
   home screen, tap the "+" FAB 3 times (add name + designation +
   station each time), then in the capture sheet at the bottom type
   one short instruction per person.
4. Mute the phone. Disable auto-lock. The user should not be
   interrupted by the screen turning off mid-flow.

## The 6 tasks, in order

Read each task to the user **once**, verbatim, then stop talking. If
they ask "what should I do next?" — say nothing. The task text is
self-contained. Watch and take notes.

### Task 1 — Capture a quick note

> "You just got off a call with the Tehsildar about a land dispute
> hearing next Tuesday. Make a note so you don't forget."

What we are watching for: do they find the capture entry point
without prompting? How long does it take? Do they default to text
or reach for the voice button?

### Task 2 — Find that note later

> "It's been a few hours. You want to check on the land dispute
> note. Find it."

What we are watching for: do they use the search bar? Do they tap
on a person? How long does it take? Do they find a way to mark it
done?

### Task 3 — Mark something done

> "The Tehsildar confirmed the hearing date. Mark that note as
> handled."

What we are watching for: do they understand the action affordance
(Mark done / Drop / Reopen)? Is the action text clear? Do they
hesitate between Mark done and Drop?

### Task 4 — Hide a sensitive person

> "You want to hide 'Person-with-many-hyphens-and-some-special-chars'
> from the home list. The person is real, you just don't want them
> visible right now."

What we are watching for: do they find the vault-mode toggle? Do
they understand what "Visible" vs "Hidden" means? Do they set a
PIN? (If they don't, note that — the data isn't actually hidden
without one.)

### Task 5 — Make a backup

> "Before you leave the office, make an encrypted backup of your
> data so you can recover it on a new phone."

What we are watching for: do they find Settings → Data → Export
encrypted vault? Do they understand the passphrase prompt (8+
chars, must match)? Do they note down the passphrase? Do they
export to a file they can find later?

### Task 6 — Restore from backup

> "Pretend you just got a new phone. Restore from the backup you
> made."

What we are watching for: do they find the import entry? Do they
type the passphrase correctly? Do they re-import the same file
twice (and notice anything weird — should be idempotent)?

## After the 30 minutes

Write up the friction points as a single document. For each
friction, give:

- **Timestamp** in the recording
- **Task number** (1–6)
- **What they did** (one sentence)
- **What they expected** (one sentence)
- **What happened instead** (one sentence)
- **Severity** (blocker / annoyance / observation)

The next v1.7.x polish pass will address all "blocker" + "annoyance"
items in priority order. "Observation" items go in the
nice-to-have backlog.

## What you do NOT do

- Do not explain the app to the user. They should discover it.
- Do not rescue them when they get stuck. If they have been stuck
  for 60 seconds, note the timestamp and ask "what are you
  thinking right now?"
- Do not show them the search bar. If they do not find it, that
  is a finding.
- Do not let them use the dev's phone. Use a phone that does
  not have their own data in it.
- Do not let them keep going past 30 minutes. Stop the recording
  and start the debrief.

## Why this test exists

The v1.6.8 fresh-eyes critique scored the app 6.5/10. The three
gaps that kept it below "ship as a colleague tool" were:

1. A real user had not used it. (closed by this test)
2. There was no backup story. (closed by v1.7.0: the export/import
   ships in v1.5.0, but never had a release note for it; v1.7.0
   is the first APK to actually exercise it on a real device
   round trip.)
3. The search bar existed but tapping a result did not jump to
   the instruction. (closed by v1.7.0: tapping an instruction
   in search results now opens the InstructionDetailSheet,
   with the same Mark done / Drop / Reopen affordances as the
   Today brief cards.)

The v1.7.0 score should be 8+ after this test. If the friction
list from this handoff is longer than 5 items, it is not.
