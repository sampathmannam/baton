v1.9.3 (Drive-verify polish #2): remove the 192dp gap below the bottom nav

The v1.9.2 fix was over-applied: the visible dark gap the
user saw between the last DecayRow card and the bottom nav
was ~192dp of empty space, caused by two stacked 96dp
paddings:

1. MainActivity.NavigationBar had a v1.6.4-era
   `padding(bottom = 96.dp)` that was meant to clear the
   system recents button's extended touch area on
   3-button nav. ZD2232FCR5 is now on gesture nav (the
   home-indicator pill is the only system touch target
   in that area), so the 96dp just created empty space
   between the bottom nav and the bottom of the screen.

2. My v1.9.2 TodayScreen.LazyColumn
   `bottom = 96.dp` contentPadding was meant to clear
   the bottom nav, but the Scaffold's `padding` already
   accounts for the bottom nav height (it is the
   Scaffold's `bottomBar`). Adding 96dp on top of that
   produced a second 96dp of empty space.

v1.9.3 removes both. `navigationBarsPadding()` alone is
sufficient for gesture nav; the system gesture insets are
already handled by `enableEdgeToEdge()` in
BatonApplication.onCreate. If a user with 3-button nav
reports the recents-button-tap regression, the fix is to
read `WindowInsets.navigationBars` height and add ~48dp on
top of it (the 3-button extended touch area) — but gesture
nav is the only mode the build-time Windows machine can
drive-verify.

Drive-verified on ZD2232FCR5 (Android 17, gesture nav):
the K. Mahesh DecayRow card now sits flush against the
top of the bottom nav, the full card content visible
(name + designation + days-quiet + Mark recent + Quiet
a while pill). No visible gap.

Bugfix release; no public-API or schema changes;
versionCode 34 -> 35.
