fix(v1.7.4): subtitle wrap+ellipsis + NoteBar clearance

Three latent People list/search bugs visible on stress-test data,
all closed in v1.7.4:

P1-A: PersonRow and HomeScreenSearchResults subtitle Text now have
  maxLines (1 and 2 respectively) + overflow=Ellipsis. Fixes (1)
  mid-word character-level breaks at hyphens ("mobi|le-screens"
  → "...mobile-screens...") and (2) truncation without ellipsis
  ("Waran" → "Waran..."). The unbounded Text was breaking at any
  character position when a long designation + station didn't fit
  the row width.

  HomeScreenSearchResults. NoteBar measures 96dp (12+72+12), FAB
  clearance was 88dp. Last row "B. Srinivas" was half-hidden
  behind the NoteBar on a 1080x2400 viewport.
